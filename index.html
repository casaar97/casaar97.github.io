01About

Hello! I'm a dedicated Data Software Engineer based in Málaga, Andalusia, Spain. With a solid foundation in both software engineering and data technologies, I am passionate about crafting innovative solutions that drive meaningful impact in our fast-paced tech world.

My journey into software development began during my degree in computer science at the University of Córdoba. Once I finished it, I became interested in the data world, so I decided to pursue a master's degree in Big Data Analytics at the University of Málaga. Since then, I've been fortunate to work on a wide array of projects, ranging from cloud computing solutions to microservices as a software engineer, and data pipelines as a data analytics engineer. I am particularly fascinated by how software engineering and data technologies intersect to create powerful tools and applications.

I am always eager to embrace new challenges and collaborate on exciting projects. Whether it's developing cutting-edge solutions, optimizing data pipelines, or designing robust cloud systems, I am ready to take on the next adventure. If you have an intriguing opportunity or simply want to connect, please feel free to reach out!
